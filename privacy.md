# Prarthanakal — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 22 September 2026_

Prarthanakal (പ്രാർത്ഥനകൾ) is a Malayalam Catholic prayer app published by its developer
("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short
version: **everything you enter stays on your phone. We run no servers or accounts and show
no advertising. The only things that leave your phone are anonymous usage statistics and
crash reports — which you can switch off, and which never include your name, your lists, your
remembrance days or your photos — and a version check against the app store.**

## What the app stores, and where

All of the following is kept only on your device, in the app's private storage, and is
deleted when you uninstall the app:

- **Your preferences** — language, theme, home layout, text size, reminder times.
- **Your prayer journal** — which prayers you marked as prayed, your streak, Rosary count
  and novena progress.
- **Your lists and favourites.**
- **Remembrance days** — names, relations and dates you add for loved ones, and any photo
  you choose for them. Photos are copied into the app's private folder and are never
  uploaded anywhere.
- **Your support history** — whether you are a Supporter and how many candles you have lit,
  so the app can thank you and stop asking. This is a simple local flag; the actual
  purchase records are held by Google Play or the App Store, not by us.
- **Nudge history** — when the app last asked for support, so it does not ask again too
  soon.

The app connects to the internet for exactly three things, each described below: the
anonymous usage statistics, the update check, and purchases handled by Google Play or the App
Store. Links you open from the app open in your browser, under your browser's rules.

## Anonymous usage statistics and crash reports

To learn which prayers and features are actually used — and so decide what to add, translate
or record next — the app can send anonymous usage statistics to **Google Firebase**
(Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics), acting as our processor.

**You choose.** The last page of the welcome screens has a checkbox, *Help improve the app
with anonymous usage statistics*, next to a link that shows exactly what is shared; you can
change your answer at any time in Settings → Privacy → *Share anonymous usage statistics*. Turning it off stops all
collection immediately, discards what the app was about to send, resets the app-instance
identifier, and changes nothing else about the app.

- **Where the law requires opt-in consent**, the box starts unticked and nothing is collected
  until you tick it; skipping the page means no. This applies in the European Union
  and EEA, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, Canada, the United Arab Emirates, Qatar, Bahrain,
  Oman, Brazil, South Korea, China, Turkey, South Africa, Thailand, Malaysia, Indonesia, the
  Philippines, Vietnam, Russia, Israel, Argentina, Egypt, Nigeria and Kenya. The app decides
  this from your phone's region and time-zone settings, and if either points to one of these
  places the opt-in rule is used.
- **Elsewhere** (including India, the United States, Australia, New Zealand, Singapore and
  Saudi Arabia) the box starts ticked; one tap clears it, on that page or later in Settings.

**What is sent when the switch is on**

- Which prayers are opened and marked as prayed, and from where in the app (Home, Library,
  search, a reminder, the widget).
- Rosary and Way of the Cross completions, prayer cards shared, read-aloud started, reminders
  tapped, lists created (how many prayers, not their names), remembrance days added (whether a
  photo or a year was added — never the name, relation or date).
- Searches that found nothing (the search text itself, so we can add what is missing). This is
  the only thing you type that is ever sent.
- Which settings are chosen (language, script, theme, Home layout, text size, reminders on or
  off), whether you are a Supporter, and the support prompts shown and answered.
- Crash reports: the technical stack trace, app version, device model and operating system
  version.
- With every item, what Firebase attaches: an app-instance identifier generated at install
  (reset when you turn the switch off or reinstall), country, device language, phone model, OS
  and app version.

**What is never sent**: your name, prayer-list names, remembrance names, relations, dates or
photos, intentions, purchase amounts, the text of any prayer, how far you read, or anything you
type other than a search that found nothing.

- Alongside a crash report, Crashlytics records the device's free memory and storage, screen
  orientation, whether the app was in the foreground, and the last few screens visited —
  the technical context needed to reproduce a fault. Crash reporting is part of the same
  switch.

**No advertising.** The Android advertising ID and Apple's advertising identifier are never
read, ad personalisation is disabled in the SDK, and the data is not used for advertising or
shared with anyone other than Google as our processor. Firebase's own terms apply to their
handling of the data: [https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy). Statistics are kept for
14 months and then deleted automatically; crash reports are kept for 90 days.

**Legal basis.** In the opt-in regions above we rely on your consent (GDPR / UK GDPR / the
equivalent local law) for the usage statistics and crash reports. Elsewhere we rely on our
legitimate interest in understanding and improving the app, balanced by the fact that the data
is anonymous, that ad identifiers are never read, and that a one-tap switch turns it off. You
may object or withdraw at any time with that switch, and the app works identically either way.

**Where it is processed.** Firebase stores this data on Google's servers, which may be outside
your country (including in the United States), under Google's data-processing terms and the
standard safeguards they provide for international transfers.

## Update check

When the app starts, and at most once an hour when you return to it, it asks the app store
whether a newer version is listed: on Android it fetches the app's public Google Play listing
page; on iOS it calls Apple's public app-lookup service. The request carries only the app's
identifier and your device's country and language; like any web request it reveals your IP
address to Google or Apple. Nothing is stored by us. If a newer version exists the app shows
you a note with the release notes; you can put it off for three days. Occasionally a version
may be marked as required, in which case the note stays until you update.

## In-app purchases

Supporter subscriptions and candles are sold and processed by Google Play (Android) or the
App Store (iOS). They handle your payment details under their own privacy policies; we
receive no personal information about you from a purchase. Nothing in the app is locked
behind a purchase.

## Notifications

If you turn on prayer, Mass or remembrance reminders, the app schedules local notifications
on your device. No notification service outside your phone is involved.

## Read-aloud

The read-aloud feature uses your phone's built-in text-to-speech engine. Depending on your
device settings that engine may be provided by Google or Apple and governed by their
policies; the app itself sends no text anywhere.

## Sharing

When you share a prayer card or a message, the app hands the content to the app you choose
(WhatsApp, Messages, etc.). What happens next is governed by that app.

## Home-screen widget and Android Auto

The widget and Android Auto surfaces show the same locally stored content; no data leaves the
device.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone,
including children. The anonymous usage statistics above contain nothing that identifies a
person, and a parent can turn them off in Settings → Privacy.

## Your rights

Because we hold nothing that identifies you, most rights are exercised on the phone itself:
everything you entered can be read, changed or deleted in the app, and turning the usage
statistics switch off both stops collection and resets the app-instance identifier, so
nothing already collected can be connected to your device again. If you are in the EU, the
UK, India or anywhere with a data-protection law that gives you rights of access, correction,
deletion or objection, write to the address below and we will act on your request within the
time the law allows (we will only be able to act on what can be found from the details you
give, as we cannot look you up by name). You may also complain to your local data-protection
authority.

## Your choices

- Turn anonymous usage statistics off in Settings → Privacy (or during setup).
- Delete any remembrance, list or favourite from within the app.
- Turn reminders off in Settings or in your phone's notification settings.
- Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.
- Manage or cancel a subscription in Google Play → Subscriptions or App Store → Subscriptions.
