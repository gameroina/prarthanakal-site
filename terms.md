## The app

Prarthanakal is a free Malayalam Catholic prayer companion. Every prayer, the Rosary, the
Way of the Cross, Yaama prayers, novenas and all other features are available to everyone
at no cost, with no advertising inside prayers. We may add, change or remove features at any
time.

## Supporter subscription and candles

You can, if you wish, support the app in two ways. Neither unlocks anything; both are a
thank-you that keeps the app free for everyone.

- **Supporter (yearly)** — an auto-renewing subscription billed once a year through
  Google Play or the App Store at the price shown in the app for your country. It renews
  automatically for the same period unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of
  the current period. You can manage or cancel it at any time in Google Play → Subscriptions
  or App Store → Subscriptions; cancelling stops future renewals and you keep the Supporter
  badge until the paid period ends.
- **Light a candle** — a one-time purchase in three sizes, as often as you like. Each
  candle is a separate purchase confirmed by the store.

Purchases are made through Google Play or the App Store under their terms; refunds are
handled by them according to their policies. Prices may differ by country and may include
tax.
